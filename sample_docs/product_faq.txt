Product FAQ - AcmeWidget Pro

## What is AcmeWidget Pro?
AcmeWidget Pro is our flagship productivity platform that combines task management, team collaboration, and analytics in one integrated workspace.

## Pricing Tiers
- **Starter**: $12/user/month - Core task management, up to 10 projects
- **Professional**: $29/user/month - Unlimited projects, advanced analytics, integrations
- **Enterprise**: Custom pricing - SSO, dedicated support, SLA guarantees, on-premise option

## Free Trial
All tiers offer a 14-day free trial with full feature access. No credit card required to start. Trial converts to Starter plan automatically unless changed.

## Integrations
AcmeWidget Pro integrates natively with: Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Jira, GitHub, GitLab, Salesforce, HubSpot, and 50+ other tools via Zapier.

## API Access
REST API available on Professional and Enterprise tiers. Rate limits: 1,000 requests/hour (Professional), 10,000 requests/hour (Enterprise). API keys managed in Settings > Developer.

## Data Security
SOC 2 Type II certified. AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. Data centers in US-East, EU-West, and AP-Southeast. GDPR and CCPA compliant. Enterprise tier offers customer-managed encryption keys.

## Support
- Starter: Email support, 48-hour response
- Professional: Email + chat, 4-hour response
- Enterprise: 24/7 phone/email/chat, 1-hour response, dedicated CSM

## Onboarding
Professional and Enterprise tiers include guided onboarding (2-4 hours). Starter tier has self-serve documentation and video tutorials.
